What the app does

  • Track any habit your way

    Yes or no, a count, a duration, or a multi check-in for habits you do 2 to 4 times a day. Schedule each habit every day, on specific days, or as a weekly target.

  • Keep the streak alive

    See your current and longest streak for every habit. Milestones at 7, 14, 21, 30, 50, 75, 100, 150, 200, 365, 500 and 1000 days get a celebration, and 16 achievement badges mark the bigger wins.

  • Pair with an accountability partner

    Send an invite link and pair for free. With Premium, each shared habit you complete in the app for today is sent to your partner the moment it happens, with milestone alerts and, when the app is started in the evening, a warning if a streak is at risk; your partner does not need Premium to receive them. Free accounts get 3 nudges a month and a combined weekly count of what you both completed.

  • Focus with a built-in timer

    A Pomodoro timer runs 25 minute work sessions with 5 minute breaks and a 15 minute long break, so a habit like reading or deep work has its time carved out.

  • Get reminders that change

    Set a reminder time per habit. The wording is picked again each time you open the app, and a habit on a streak of 7 or more days can get a different message for each of the next few days, within the limit iOS sets on waiting notifications.

  • Glance from the home screen

    Interactive widgets show today's habits, your progress and streaks, and a partner widget shows how their day is going when they have Premium. Tap to complete without opening the app. A habit completed from a widget sends your partner no alert, though it still adds to the weekly count.

Free to start, one payment to unlock everything

The free version includes 5 habits, all tracking types, streaks, badges, widgets, iCloud sync, Apple Health and a partner with 3 nudges a month. Premium is a one-time purchase of $9.99, not a subscription, and adds unlimited habits, habit stacking, smart insights, real-time partner updates and unlimited partner nudges.

Questions

Is HabitFlame free?
Yes. The free version tracks up to 5 habits with every tracking type, streaks, badges, widgets, reminders, the focus timer, iCloud sync, Apple Health and an accountability partner with 3 nudges a month. Premium is a one-time purchase of $9.99 that removes the habit limit and adds habit stacking, smart insights, real-time partner updates and unlimited nudges.
Do I need an account?
No. HabitFlame works the moment you open it. Your habits are stored on your device and in your own iCloud. With Premium, the shared habits you complete are delivered to your partner through HabitFlame's pairing service and Apple push notifications. Without Premium, the service carries your nudges and your reactions, which name the habit you react to, and, for the weekly count, the day of each completion with a one-way hash of the habit's identifier and no habit name. What pairing itself stores is set out in the privacy policy. Pairing with a partner uses an invite link, not a username or password.
Which devices does it run on?
HabitFlame is an iPhone and iPad app from the App Store. There is no Android or web version.
Does my partner need to pay?
No. Pairing, reactions and nudges are free for both of you. Real-time partner updates depend on Premium on the side of the person whose completions are sent: if you have Premium, your partner gets each shared habit you complete in the app for today instantly without paying. With both of you on the free version, you see a combined weekly count of what you completed and can nudge each other.
