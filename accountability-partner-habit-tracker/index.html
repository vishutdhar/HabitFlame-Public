A habit tracker with a real accountability partner

Most habit apps track. HabitFlame adds the one thing that changes whether you show up: another person who can see whether you did.

Why a partner beats a reminder

A reminder tells you what to do. It does not care if you ignore it. A person does. When you know your sister will see that you skipped your walk again, the walk gets taken. That social pull is the reason running clubs, study groups and gym buddies work, and it is the reason most solo habit apps get deleted after a few weeks.

HabitFlame was built to put that pull inside a habit tracker without turning it into a social network. There is no feed of strangers, no public profile and no score to compare against people you do not know. There is one partner, or a few, and the habits you choose to share with each of them.

How pairing works

You add a partner from the Partners tab and send them an invite link by message, email or any share sheet destination. When they open the link in HabitFlame, the two of you are paired. If they do not have the app yet, the link takes them to the App Store first. No usernames, no passwords and no account sign-up on either side.

For each partner you decide which habits to share. Share everything, or only the one habit you keep failing at. Your partner sees the shared habits, whether you completed them today and your streak on each. Your unshared habits stay private.

What your partner can do

  • See your shared habits and whether each one is done today.
  • React to a completion to say well done, which shows up in your activity feed.
  • Send you a nudge when a habit is still open, which arrives as a notification on your phone.
  • Keep their own habits in the same app, so the accountability runs both ways.

Free accounts can send 3 nudges a month, enough to feel the feature. Premium removes the limit, so a partner who wants to poke you every evening can.

Real-time updates

With Premium, real-time partner updates send your partner an instant notification the moment you complete a shared habit, and you get the same when they complete theirs. That is the version most people picture when they hear accountability partner: your phone buzzes, it says they did their workout, and now it is your turn. Without Premium, your partner's progress still shows in the app and in the partner widget when you open them, it just does not push to you at the moment it happens.

The partner widget

A home screen widget shows how your partner's day is going next to your own. It is a small thing, but seeing an unfinished day on someone you care about, right on your home screen, tends to end with a nudge and a completed habit on both sides.

What stays private

HabitFlame does not require an account, and your habit data lives on your device and in your own iCloud. Pairing with a partner shares only the habits you pick and only their completion status, streak and reactions. There is no location sharing, no message history and nothing your partner can see beyond what you chose to share.

Everything else in the tracker

The partner feature sits on top of a full habit tracker. You can log a habit as yes or no, as a count such as glasses of water, as a duration such as minutes of reading, or as a multi check-in for something you do 2 to 4 times a day. Each habit runs every day, on specific days of the week or against a weekly target. Streaks, milestone celebrations, 16 achievement badges, home and lock screen widgets, a Pomodoro focus timer, stats with a full year view, and reminders that change their wording are all included.

Choosing the right partner

The best accountability partner is not the most motivated person you know. It is someone who will actually notice, and who you would feel a little awkward letting down. A friend who checks their phone every evening beats a coach you speak to once a month. A sibling, a housemate or a colleague building the same kind of habit all work well.

Agree on the rules before you start. Decide which habits you will share, whether nudges are welcome every day or only when a streak is at risk, and what counts as done. A habit like reading for 30 minutes is clear. A habit like eating better is not, and a vague habit is hard for anyone to hold you to. Start with one or two shared habits, get a few weeks of streaks behind you, then add more.

Questions about accountability partners

Does my accountability partner need to buy Premium?
No. Both of you can use the free version and pair. Premium only changes what the person who bought it gets: unlimited habits, unlimited nudges, real-time partner updates, habit stacking and smart insights.
Can I have more than one accountability partner?
Yes. You can pair with several people and choose which habits each of them sees.
What does my partner see?
Only the habits you share with them: whether each is done today, your streak on it, and reactions and nudges between you. Habits you do not share stay private.
Does my partner have to be on iPhone?
Yes. HabitFlame is an iPhone and iPad app, so both people need an Apple device with the app installed.
What happens if my partner does not open the invite?
Nothing changes on your side. Your habits keep tracking as normal, and you can resend the link or invite someone else.
