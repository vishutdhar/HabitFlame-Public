Support

Answers to the questions people ask most, and how to reach us.

Email support@freedom-terminal.com with any question about HabitFlame. We typically reply within 24 to 48 hours.

Frequently asked questions

How do I add an accountability partner?
Open the Partners tab and send an invite link, show a QR code, or pick someone from your contacts. When your partner opens the link with HabitFlame installed, the app shows your invite and they tap Accept to pair. If they do not have the app yet, the invite page links to the App Store and shows the invite code, which they enter in the Partners tab after installing. An invite expires after 7 days.
I have an invite code. Where do I enter it?
Open HabitFlame, go to the Partners tab, choose Have a code and type the 6 character code from your invite. An invite expires after 7 days, so if a code no longer works, ask your partner to send a new invite.
How do I create a new habit?
Tap the + button in the top right corner of the Today tab. Fill in your habit details, choose an icon and color, set your schedule, and tap Save.
What happens if I miss a day?
For a habit that runs every day or on specific days, your streak resets only when you miss a day the habit is scheduled, and days it is not scheduled do not count against it. For a weekly target, the streak ends only when a week finishes short of its target. Your longest streak stays on record, and your history and stats are kept, so you can always see your overall progress.
How does Apple Health auto-complete work?
For habits tied to activity, such as steps or workouts, you can let HabitFlame read the matching Apple Health data and complete the habit automatically. You choose what to allow, and you can turn access off at any time in the Health app or in iOS Settings.
How do I restore my purchase?
Open Settings, tap the upgrade row at the top of the list, then tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID you used for the original purchase.
Is my data backed up?
Your data is stored on your device, and iCloud sync is included for everyone. It happens automatically, and you turn it on or off in the iOS Settings app under iCloud.
How do I export my data?
Open Settings and choose Export Data. You can export in JSON format for backups or in CSV format for spreadsheets.
Can I use HabitFlame on multiple devices?
Yes. iCloud sync is included for everyone and keeps your habits in sync across your iPhone and iPad automatically. You turn it on or off in the iOS Settings app under iCloud.
