Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a new habit?

Tap the + button in the top right corner of the Today tab. Fill in your habit details, choose an icon and color, set your schedule, and tap Save.

What happens if I miss a day?

Your streak resets only when you miss a day the habit is scheduled. Days the habit is not scheduled do not count against it, and your longest streak stays on record. Your history and stats are kept, so you can always see your overall progress.

How do I restore my purchase?

Go to Settings > Upgrade to Premium > Restore Purchase. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you used for the original purchase.

Is my data backed up?

Your data is stored on your device, and iCloud sync is included for everyone. It happens automatically, and you turn it on or off in the iOS Settings app under iCloud.

How do I export my data?

Go to Settings > Export Data. You can export in JSON format (for backups) or CSV format (for spreadsheets).

Can I use HabitFlame on multiple devices?

Yes. iCloud sync is included for everyone and keeps your habits in sync across your iPhone and iPad automatically. You turn it on or off in the iOS Settings app under iCloud.
